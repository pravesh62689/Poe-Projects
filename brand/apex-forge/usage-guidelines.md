# Apex Forge Technology Brand Usage Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-09-08  
**Scope:** Digital assets, web properties, documentation, social cards, and external platform profiles.

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Personality

- **Name:** Apex Forge Technology
- **Product Suite:** Apex Forge Tools
- **Core Archetype:** The Master Craftsman & Systems Engineer.
- **Attributes:** Precise, dependable, mathematically disciplined, transparent, high-performance.
- **Visual Motif:** Abstract geometric forged apex combining structural slate facets with a vibrant forged-copper core and upward keystone spark.

---

## 2. Logo & Mark Clearspace Rules

1. **Clearspace Boundary:** Always maintain a minimum clearspace equal to the width of the central apex keystone ($X$) on all sides of the mark and horizontal logo.
2. **Minimum Reproducible Sizes:**
   - Standalone Favicon (`apex-forge-favicon.svg`): Minimum $16 \times 16$ px.
   - Primary Mark (`apex-forge-mark.svg`): Minimum $32 \times 32$ px.
   - Horizontal Lockup (`apex-forge-logo.svg`): Minimum width $160$ px.
3. **Prohibited Treatments:**
   - Do not skew, rotate, or stretch the aspect ratio.
   - Do not apply heavy drop shadows or rainbow gradient strokes.
   - Do not replace the custom typography with decorative display fonts.
   - Do not place the dark lockup on unapproved low-contrast backgrounds.

---

## 3. Product Icon Co-Branding Hierarchy

Individual Poe bots maintain their unique, high-contrast product icons:
- **Apex Forge OCR:** Retains `brand/ocr-doc-bot-1024.png` (document scan grid with reticle).
- **Apex Forge Regex:** Retains `brand/regex-bot-1024.png` (code delimiter slash `/.../` with lightning spark).
- **Apex Forge SQL:** Retains `brand/sql-bot-1024.png` (relational database cylinder stack with verified checkmark).

The parent company mark (`apex-forge-mark.svg`) represents the parent engineering brand and must never displace the individual product icons on their respective landing pages.
