# Community Engagement Guidelines & Zero-Spam Policy

**Author:** Head of Community & Brand Ethics Officer  
**Scope:** Strict engagement standards for team members participating in technical developer forums, Reddit subreddits, Discord servers, and StackOverflow.

---

## 1. Zero-Spam Ethical Invariants

### 1.1 Strictly Prohibited Actions (Zero Tolerance)
- **No Unsolicited Promotion:** Never create a thread whose sole purpose is promoting a bot link without prior community moderator permission.
- **No Fake Accounts or Astro-Turfing:** Never create sock-puppet accounts to ask artificial questions or praise our bots.
- **No Automated Forum Commenting:** Never script or automate replies, upvotes, or social mentions.
- **No Link Farming or Keyword Commenting:** Never leave low-effort comments containing links.

### 1.2 The "Solution-First" Invariant
You may only reference or link to our Poe bots if **all four conditions are satisfied**:
1. The user has asked a specific, unsolved technical question (e.g. *"How do I write a regex to validate international phone numbers without ReDoS?"*).
2. You provide the **complete, fully working answer in the body of your comment**, with no paywall or forced link click required to get the value.
3. The link to our tool is included merely as a transparent, free reference to test or inspect the solution live.
4. You explicitly disclose your association with the project (`"Disclaimer: I helped build this free Poe bot"`).

| **r/regex** | Complex regex construction & debugging | Answering edge-case questions; sharing ReDoS analysis. |

| **r/Accounting & r/IndiaTax** | Receipt & invoice bookkeeping workflows | Explaining GSTIN validation logic and receipt scanning tips. |
| **r/webdev & r/learnprogramming** | Junior developer learning | Answering beginner SQL and regex conceptual hurdles. |
| **StackOverflow** | Highly specific programming questions | Direct code answers meeting StackOverflow formatting standards. |
