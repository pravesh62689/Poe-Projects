## 2. Copy Guidelines for Apex Forge Technology

1. **Precision Over Hype:** Never use words like "revolutionary", "game-changing", "magic", or "instant".
2. **Disclose Limitations Contextually:** Every product page must include a dedicated "Limitations & Best Practices" section (e.g. OCR blur rejection thresholds, SQLite in-memory scope, Regex structural matching vs external mailbox verification).
3. **Evidence-Based Proof:** Features are described by *how* they work (e.g. projection profile deskew, V8 isolate execution, WASM SQLite engine).
