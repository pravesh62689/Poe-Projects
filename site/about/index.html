About Apex Forge Technology

Apex Forge Technology is an engineering-first organization focused on creating dependable, verifiable AI utilities that solve practical document and data challenges without hype or hidden data capture.

The Engineering Philosophy: Proof Over Prediction

Generic language models often fail on precision engineering tasks: they hallucinate database schema columns, generate regular expressions vulnerable to catastrophic ReDoS backtracking, and invent totals when reading faded receipts.

At Apex Forge Technology, we bridge the gap between conversational interfaces and deterministic execution sandboxes. Our tools do not guess answers—they compile, run, verify, and reconcile results in real-time execution environments before returning them to the user.

The Apex Forge Product Suite

  • Apex Forge OCR (@OCR-Doc-Parser): Executes image pre-processing (Laplacian blur checks, projection profile deskewing), optical recognition, and arithmetic line-item reconciliation.
  • Apex Forge Regex (@Regex-Gen-Tester): Compiles regular expressions in a secure V8 edge isolate, runs them against your test cases, and checks patterns for catastrophic backtracking.
  • Apex Forge SQL (@English-To-SQL): Instantiates an in-memory SQLite WebAssembly sandbox from your schema, runs the generated SQL, auto-corrects runtime errors, and previews tabular data.

Zero-Retention Privacy Commitment

We operate on a zero-recurring budget invariant on global edge networks (Cloudflare Workers and Render). We retain zero customer documents, zero database schemas, and zero personal credentials. Memory buffers are deallocated immediately upon query completion.
