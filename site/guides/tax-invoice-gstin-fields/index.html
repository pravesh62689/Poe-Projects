Essential Tax Invoice OCR Fields: Numbers, Dates & GSTIN

Automating B2B invoice processing requires extracting and validating mandatory statutory fields to claim input tax credits (ITC) and satisfy audit compliance.

1. Indian GSTIN 15-Character Structure

A Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN) follows a strict statutory format:

[ 2 Digits State ][ 10 Alphanumeric PAN ][ 1 Entity ][ Z ][ 1 Check Digit ]

Evaluating this pattern through automated regex validation eliminates invoice transcription errors.

2. Mandatory Invoice Fields Checklist

  • Invoice Number: Unique alphanumeric identifier.
  • Invoice Date: Standardized ISO 8601 calendar date (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
  • Tax Breakdown: Split rows for CGST, SGST, and IGST amounts.
  • Total Amount: Gross payable sum reconciled against line items.
